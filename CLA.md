## How to sign

Signing is handled automatically. When you open your first pull request, a bot
comments with the text below; replying to that comment with it records your
signature against your GitHub account, and you will not be asked again for
subsequent pull requests.

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Signatures are stored in this repository's `cla-signatures` branch. There is no
third-party service involved and no email address is collected beyond what is
already public in your git commits.
